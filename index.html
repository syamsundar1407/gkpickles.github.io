About Us

GK Pickles brings the authentic taste of traditional Andhra homemade pickles to your family. Backed by over 7 years of experience in preparing homemade pickles, we are proud to share the rich flavors and traditions that have been loved by family, friends, and customers over the years.

At GK Pickles, every batch is prepared using carefully selected ingredients, traditional recipes, and time-tested homemade methods passed down through generations. Our journey began with a passion for creating authentic Andhra pickles at home, and today we are excited to bring those same flavors to a wider audience through GK Pickles.
